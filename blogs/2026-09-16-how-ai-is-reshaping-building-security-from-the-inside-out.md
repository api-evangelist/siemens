Artificial intelligence is evolving rapidly. While generative AI has captured much of the recent attention, a new generation of AI is beginning to deliver measurable value across service industries: agentic AI. Unlike traditional AI systems that analyse information and provide recommendations, agentic AI can independently plan, prioritise and execute tasks within defined boundaries.
