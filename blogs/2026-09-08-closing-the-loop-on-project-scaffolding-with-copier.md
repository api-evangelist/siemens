Standards that ship, foundations that evolve: Copier distributes engineering and organizational standards through versioned templates and reconciles shared improvements with project-specific changes. Starting a software project requires establishing a project foundation for its development and evolution. To establish this foundation, teams must decide how the project will be structured, developed, validated, maintained, and delivered.
