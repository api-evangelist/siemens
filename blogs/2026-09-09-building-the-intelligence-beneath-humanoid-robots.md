Humanoid robots are moving from science fiction toward practical industrial application. Advances in AI, vision systems and tactile sensing are helping robots interpret complex environments and respond with greater dexterity. But their long-term success will depend less on how human they look and more on the intelligent systems beneath the surface – especially the semiconductors that allow them to act.
